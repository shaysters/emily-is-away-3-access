# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026-11-12)

The first release.

### Added

- Speech and braille through Prism, with every line also written to the log.
- The fake installer: every page, the license check box, the system and desktop radio buttons with descriptions of the four desktop pictures, the install progress messages and the export alert.
- Browse-mode reading of every Facenook page and pop-up: Up and Down through text, links, buttons, text boxes and described pictures; Enter presses; pages are read when they load; new parts of a page are read when they appear.
- The 2020 home page at the start: named reaction buttons, each new post read, the date rolling back to 2008.
- Sign-up: named text boxes with letter echo, Randomize links, the birthday drop-down lists, form errors, and described profile pictures.
- Profile tabs, the chat bar's tabs, notifications and friend requests.
- Chats: messages, "is typing...", reply choices, each word as it's typed, erased text, the finished message, and keys to review the chat, repeat the choices, open links and profiles. Tab opens the waiting chat when none is open.
- Posts (status, wall posts, comments): the three choices, typing, and "Posted".
- Photos: all 237 photos in the game are described, with the caption and whether you're in the photo, as thumbnails and in the photo viewer. Photos that share a picture read their own caption.
- Profile pictures say whose they are and what they show (also greyed-out and moustache versions, and the three secret avatars); the secret apps' and websites' icons are described.
- Chapters 2 to 5 checked from start to end on both Emily's and Evelyn's stories.
- Notes: the survey reads each question with its answer choices, and says when the note is ready to post.
- Quizzes: the permission pop-up, each question read as it appears, the answers as radio buttons, and the results.
- Virtual gifts on birthdays: the gifts are radio buttons, and gift pictures on walls are described.
- The secret apps: CarrotVille's plots say what's growing and when it's ready, the watering can and seeds are radio buttons, and the seed shop says prices; the dog can be petted with a button.
- The account list's Log Out button, pokes, relationship requests, photo tags, friend requests sent from profiles, and friends' full names.
- Chapter 4's log-in as Mat: the log-in form, its errors, and logging back in.
- Events: who's attending, maybe, not attending and awaiting, the RSVP buttons, and the event wall.
- The ending: the credits, the Thanks for playing page with its photos, and Your Story (your choices, and other players' percentages when the game is connected to Steam).
- The main menu after each chapter reads each new post as it's added; timeline filters are radio buttons; Photos, Playlists, Storyline and Secrets (the secret code box and what it unlocks); the save slots; the Replay pop-up; the game's Options pop-up with named volume sliders.
- Quiz results are read once they've finished counting up.
- Settings list (m), help (h), repeat (r). Settings include reading your own sent messages, friends coming online and going offline, secret hints (spoilers, off at first) and update announcements.
- New Steam achievements are announced.
- An installer, and an announcement on the main menu when a newer version is out.
